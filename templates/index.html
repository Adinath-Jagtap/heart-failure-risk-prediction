Critical Biomarkers Overview

Ejection Fraction
Percentage of blood leaving heart per contraction. Primary indicator of systolic heart failure.
Serum Creatinine
Blood creatinine level measuring glomerular filtration and renal health in cardiac patients.
Serum Sodium
Essential electrolyte; hyponatremia (<135 mEq/L) strongly correlates with severe prognosis.
